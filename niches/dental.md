## Who visits and why
- **Pain/emergency (highest intent):** toothache, broken tooth, swelling. On a phone, wants "can I be seen today?" and a number to tap. Decides in seconds.
- **New to the area / switching dentist:** compares 2-4 clinics. Checks location, hours, price, reviews and whether the dentist seems kind.
- **Cosmetic shoppers** (veneers, whitening, aligners, implants): big spend, research for weeks. Want real results, the doctor's credentials, a price range and a free or cheap consult.
- **Parents** booking for kids: want gentle, patient, child-friendly care.
- **Anxious avoiders:** haven't been in years, embarrassed, afraid of pain. Need reassurance before anything else.
- **Medical tourists / expats** (Egypt): compare price against home, need English, a WhatsApp contact and a treatment-plus-stay timeline.
- Main worries: pain, hidden costs, being upsold, hygiene and sterilisation, wait time, "will they judge my teeth".

## Must-have on the site
- [ ] Clinic name + area/city + what they do in the hero (e.g. "Family & cosmetic dentistry in Heliopolis")
- [ ] Tap-to-call phone and WhatsApp button visible above the fold on mobile (375px)
- [ ] Sticky mobile bar or header with Call / WhatsApp / Book
- [ ] One clear primary CTA in the hero, repeated after services, reviews and at the end
- [ ] Services list grouped (e.g. Everyday: checkups, cleaning, fillings, root canal, extraction / Cosmetic: whitening, veneers, aligners / Implants & crowns / Kids / Emergency)
- [ ] Emergency/same-day line ("Toothache? Call now, same-day slots when available")
- [ ] Lead dentist: real photo, name, degree, university, syndicate membership, years of practice
- [ ] Real reviews with first name + treatment (Google rating badge if they have one)
- [ ] Real clinic photos: reception, treatment room, equipment, team (no stock)
- [ ] Prices or "from" ranges for common treatments, or at least consult/checkup fee
- [ ] Payment methods (cash, card, InstaPay, installments if offered) and insurance accepted
- [ ] Hours (incl. Friday/Saturday status), full address, embedded map, parking/landmark note
- [ ] Hygiene/sterilisation statement (autoclave, single-use tools)
- [ ] FAQ: pain, cost, how long, first-visit process, kids, emergencies
- [ ] Booking form of 3 fields max: name, phone, preferred time (+ optional treatment)
- [ ] Before/after gallery only with patient consent and a "results vary" note
- [ ] Footer: NAP (name, address, phone) repeated, social links, hours

## Section order that works
1. **Header:** logo, short nav (Services, Doctor, Prices, Reviews, Contact), Book button, phone
2. **Hero:** specific headline + area, one line of reassurance, primary CTA (Book/WhatsApp) + secondary (Call), a real clinic or doctor photo, rating badge under the buttons
3. **Trust strip:** Google rating, years open, patients treated, syndicate/university logos (only real numbers)
4. **Services:** 4-8 cards in groups, each with a one-line plain-language benefit and a "from" price if available
5. **Emergency band:** short, high-contrast, call button
6. **Meet the doctor(s):** photo, credentials, a short human quote about their approach
7. **Why us / first visit:** 3-4 steps of what happens (consult, X-ray, clear plan and price before treatment, treatment), plus the comfort points (painless anaesthesia, sterilisation)
8. **Results:** before/after (consented) or smile gallery
9. **Reviews:** 3-6 real reviews, link to Google
10. **Prices & payment:** table or ranges, payment methods, installments
11. **FAQ**
12. **Location & hours:** map, address, landmark, hours, contact buttons
13. **Final CTA + footer**

## Trust signals
- The doctor's face and name. People choose a dentist, not a clinic. Real portrait, not in a stock pose.
- Credentials stated plainly: BDS/master's, university (Cairo, Ain Shams, Alexandria, AUC/BUE, foreign fellowships), syndicate membership, years in practice.
- Google rating + count, with review quotes that name the treatment ("my root canal didn't hurt at all").
- Real photos of the clinic, especially a clean treatment room and sterilisation area.
- Named technology only if it's real: digital X-ray/CBCT, intraoral scanner, CEREC/same-day crowns, rotary endo, laser.
- Price transparency: "You get the full plan and price before we start" is the line patients repeat in reviews.
- Before/after photos taken by the clinic (same lighting/angle, unedited).
- Warranty on implants/crowns if the clinic offers one.
- Kids: photos of the kid-friendly corner, a gentle approach line.

## Primary call to action
- **Egypt/MENA default: WhatsApp first, call second.** "Book on WhatsApp" opens `https://wa.me/<number>?text=` with a prefilled message ("Hi, I'd like to book a checkup at <clinic>").
- Secondary: tap-to-call (`tel:`). Tertiary: short booking form or Vezeeta/booking-system link if the clinic uses one.
- Placement: header (desktop), sticky bottom bar on mobile (WhatsApp + Call), hero, after services, after reviews, final section, contact section.
- Emergency CTA is always "Call now" (fastest path), not a form.
- Cosmetic CTA: "Book a smile consultation" (mention if free or the fee).
- Labels: verbs + outcome ("Book a checkup", "WhatsApp us", "Call the clinic"). Not "Submit" or "Learn more".
- Tap targets of 44px or more; the phone number must be a link, never an image.

## Copy angles
- Lead with location + specific service: "Gentle family dentistry in Nasr City, open until 10 pm."
- Answer the fear: "Scared of the dentist? Most of our patients were. We explain every step and you can stop us any time."
- Price honesty: "Clear price before any treatment. No surprises."
- Speed/emergency: "Toothache? Same-day appointments when you call before 6 pm."
- Doctor-led: "Every treatment by Dr. <Name>, 15 years in implants and restorative dentistry."
- Concrete outcome: "Straighter teeth in 6-12 months with clear aligners. Free scan to see your result first."
- First visit: "Your first visit: 30-minute check, X-ray, and a written plan. <fee> EGP."
- Kids: "Kids' first visits that end in a sticker, not tears."
- Turn reviews into headings: quote a real line ("I didn't feel the injection.").
- Keep it short, warm and plain. Say "tooth-coloured filling", not "composite restoration".
- Never promise "painless" outright or guaranteed results; say "comfortable", "gentle", "we numb fully before we start".

## Generic clichés to avoid
- Stock photos: model with perfect white smile, gloved hand holding a mirror, giant tooth 3D render, laughing family on white, toothbrush close-ups.
- Tooth-icon logos and tooth icons on every service card.
- "Your smile is our priority" / "State-of-the-art facility" / "Creating beautiful smiles" / "Welcome to our website" / "Your trusted dental partner".
- Generic teal + white + light blue palette with no brand reason (use the client's brand colors only).
- Image sliders/carousels in the hero; autoplaying video with sound.
- Huge service lists of 25 clinical terms with no prices or explanations.
- "Contact us" as the only CTA, buried at the bottom; long forms asking for insurance and history up front.
- Fake numbers ("10,000+ happy smiles") without a source.
- Pop-ups on mobile covering the call button.
- Unconsented or obviously stock before/after photos.

## Local notes
- **Language:** Egypt clinics need Arabic + English. Arabic (RTL) for local families; English for expats, tourists and upper-middle-class patients. If bilingual, mirror the layout with `dir="rtl"` and an Arabic font, and keep phone numbers LTR. Egyptian colloquial is fine for CTAs ("احجز على واتساب"); keep medical copy in MSA-light.
- **WhatsApp is the booking channel.** Most patients would rather message than call or fill a form. Use the clinic's WhatsApp Business number in international format (+20...). Show the number as text too.
- **Payment:** cash still common; add card, InstaPay, Vodafone Cash/wallets, and installments (valU, Sympl, bank 0% plans) if the clinic accepts them. List insurance/medical networks (AXA, Allianz, MetLife, company contracts) if applicable.
- **Prices:** in EGP. Many clinics avoid publishing prices; at minimum show the consultation (kashf) fee. Tourists want EUR/USD ranges.
- **Hours:** evening clinics (often 4-11 pm) and Friday closures are normal; state them clearly. Ramadan hours note if relevant.
- **Location:** give a landmark and floor/building ("3rd floor, above X pharmacy"); Google Maps link, not just embed.
- **Vezeeta / Google Business:** many patients find dentists on Vezeeta; link the profile and show its rating if strong. Keep NAP identical to Google Business.
- **Regulation (Egypt):** Medical Syndicate ethics restrict misleading medical advertising. A 2026 syndicate draft bans sharing patient images/data without explicit written consent, using children in medical ads, and claiming unverified outcomes. So: consented before/afters only, no identifiable children's faces without consent, no "guaranteed"/"best in Egypt" claims. Gulf clients (e.g. Dubai DHA) have explicit rules: unedited same-patient before/afters with a "results vary" disclaimer.
- **Privacy:** don't collect medical history on the site; take it at the clinic or in WhatsApp.
- **Medical tourism clients:** add an English section on the process (consult online, how many days of stay, airport pickup if offered) and a WhatsApp CTA tuned to it.
